## Exclusions and unresolved details
- APMA 2027 is Washington, DC; ACFAS 2027 is Orlando; Midwest Foot & Ankle is Chicago. Participation by a four-state association does not make these local shows.
- AMIFAS's 2027 annual cadaver lab is Charlotte, despite stale directories naming New Orleans. Its latest New Orleans masters course has no verified next local date or commercial booth offer.
- DFCon Anaheim is distinct from San Antonio DFUpdate. TexFix and IEFS are one continuing series. LPMA and the French Quarter Conference are one joint meeting. PFA and AAOP share one exhibition.
- Patient-treatment clinics such as Mission of Mercy, board administration, purely virtual meetings, certification classes and unverified academic course listings are not added as trade conferences.
- Arkansas's old Little Rock podiatry brochure is from 2023, not an announced 2027 event.
- TPMA Southwest's final vendor-day row has a weekday/date conflict. Confirm corrected vendor hours and required official hotel-block terms.
- ACPA December, TPMA Annual and other events publish prior-day setup windows; confirm opening-day setup permission. The user's no-before/no-after hotel policy remains unchanged.
- Commercial access is unverified for some clinical courses and smaller CE meetings; these are labeled clearly. A booth is not promised merely because a conference is listed.
- This review covers publicly discoverable organizer calendars as of the check date. New 2027 announcements, private seminars and local meetings can appear later.
